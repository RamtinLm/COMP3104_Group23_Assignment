George Brown Polytechnic

George Brown Polytechnic, formerly known as George Brown College, is a public educational institution located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
It offers different programs in areas such as technology, business, health sciences, and design. 
The institution focuses on practical education and helping students prepare for their future careers.

Campuses and Programs

George Brown Polytechnic has different campuses in Toronto, including Casa Loma Campus, St. James Campus, and Waterfront Campus.
 Each campus offers different programs and learning facilities. 
Students can study subjects such as computer programming, business, construction, hospitality, and health sciences. 
The college also provides hands-on learning opportunities to help students develop practical skills.
