Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a process that developers follow to create and maintain software applications. 
It helps teams organize their work and develop software in a structured way.
The main phases of SDLC include planning, requirements analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance.
During planning, the team identifies the project goals. 
In the requirements phase, developers understand what users need. The design phase focuses on how the application will work. 
Developers then write the code, test the application, and deploy it for users. After deployment, the software needs regular updates and maintenance.
