George Brown Polytechnic is a public college located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It was previously known as George Brown College. The college offers different programs in areas like technology, business, health sciences, and design. George Brown focuses on practical learning and helps students develop skills for their future careers.

George Brown Polytechnic has different campuses in Toronto, including Casa Loma Campus, St. James Campus, and Waterfront Campus. Each campus offers different programs and facilities for students. Casa Loma Campus is known for programs in technology, engineering, and construction. The college also provides hands-on learning opportunities to help students gain practical experience.
