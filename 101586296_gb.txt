Introduction to George Brown Polytechnic:

George Brown Polytechnic is a public post-secondary school located in the heart of downtown Toronto. It recently changed its name from George Brown College to George Brown Polytechnic to better reflect the wide range of programs it offers, including bachelor's degrees and graduate certificates alongside diplomas. The school is known for practical, hands-on training and strong partnerships with industry, which help students move directly from the classroom into the workforce.

Campuses and program areas:

George Brown is spread across three main campuses in downtown Toronto. St. James Campus, close to the historic St. Lawrence neighbourhood, is known for business, hospitality and culinary programs. Casa Loma Campus, in the north part of downtown, focuses on technology, construction and design. Waterfront Campus, right by Lake Ontario, is the centre for health sciences. Each campus has its own feel, but they are all close enough to reach by TTC.
