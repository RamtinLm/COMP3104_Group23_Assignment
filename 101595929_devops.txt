COMP3104 DevOps is a course I am taking in the third year of the Computer Programming and Analysis program at George Brown Polytechnic, taught by Pritesh Patel in Fall 2026. The course introduces DevOps as a way of working that brings development and operations teams together, so software can be built, tested and released faster and more reliably. It combines lectures with handsOn labs, which means most concepts are practised directly in the terminal and on GitHub.

The course covers a wide range of topics. We started with Bash and the command line, then moved on to version control with Git and GitHub, branching workflows and pull requests. Later weeks focus on CI/CD pipelines with tools such as GitHub Actions, Travis CI and Jenkins, along with cloud computing, infrastructure as code, automated testing with SonarQube and Selenium, and containerization with Docker. It also looks at the bigger picture, such as monolith versus microservices architectures and the different roles inside a DevOps team.
