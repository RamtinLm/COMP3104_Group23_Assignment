George Brown Polytechnic is a public college of applied arts and technology in downtown Toronto. It opened in 1967 and now has three campuses across the city. The school is named after George Brown, a journalist and politician who founded The Globe newspaper in 1844 and played an important role in shaping Canada.

In October 2025, George Brown College officially changed its name to George Brown Polytechnic. The new name reflects its focus on career-ready programs, hands-on learning and applied research, and the school consistently ranks among the top research colleges in Canada.
